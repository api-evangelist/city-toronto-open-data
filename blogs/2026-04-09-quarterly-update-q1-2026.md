Toronto Open Data experienced steady growth in Q1 2026, with 88 data requests received and dataset downloads up 6% compared to the previous year. The team published 3 new datasets, significantly updated 40+ existing ones, and launched platform improvements including a redesigned homepage and enhanced dataset explorer.
