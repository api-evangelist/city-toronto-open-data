rideTO is a mobile app designed to help Toronto Transit Commission commuters monitor their regular routes by consolidating real-time arrival data, service alerts, and accessibility outages into a single interface. The app uses Toronto's open data to reduce commuter cognitive load by eliminating the need to check multiple sources for transit information.
